## 1. The First End-to-End MVP Workflow

The MVP focuses on executing **one complete, bulletproof property-investigation workflow** for Bihar:

* **Step 1: Create Investigation:** User initiates a property diligence request in the web portal.
* **Step 2: Collect Location & Parcel Identifiers:** User provides District, Anchal, Mauza, and either Rayat Name, Khata, or Khesra (Plot) Number.
* **Step 3: Resolve Property Identity:** System resolves candidates and binds an immutable `canonical_property_id` (e.g. `BR-PAT-DAN-KOT-KH48-PL112`).
* **Step 4: Retrieve Official Records:** Automated scraper fetches digitized **Jamabandi Register-II** from Bihar Bhumi (`emutation.bihar.gov.in`) and cadastral boundary from **Bhu-Naksha**.
* **Step 5: Document Ingestion:** User or attorney uploads existing registered deed scans or historical Khatiyan documents.
* **Step 6: Structured Field Extraction:** Document OCR extracts recorded parties, deed numbers, dates, Chauhaddi, and area.
* **Step 7: Cross-Document Comparison:** Consistency Engine compares deed declarations against revenue Jamabandi records and cadastral map boundaries.
* **Step 8: Identify Discrepancies & Gaps:** Engine automatically generates categorized findings (e.g. area deficit, seller name variance, unverified pre-digitization links).
* **Step 9: LandAmin Field Routing:** Unresolved boundary or possession questions are dispatched as a field task to a certified local LandAmin.
* **Step 10: Generate Land Intelligence Report:** System compiles an audit-ready, 8-page diligence report with evidence citations and legal disclaimers.
* **Step 11: Persist in Property Passport:** The entire dossier is archived in a persistent Property Passport for continuous monitoring.

---

## 2. Explicit MVP Exclusions

To ensure rapid, flawless delivery, the following are strictly **excluded** from the initial release:
1. **Nationwide Source Coverage:** Excluded. Coverage is restricted strictly to Bihar during the pilot.
2. **Autonomous Legal Clearance:** Excluded. Automated reports state factual findings and discrepancies; they do not issue autonomous legal title warranties.
3. **Complex Multi-Agent Swarms:** Excluded. The MVP utilizes deterministic rules and structured vision LLM pipelines rather than uncontrollable autonomous swarms.
4. **Automated Scraping of Captcha-Protected Financial Gateways:** Excluded. CERSAI and bank charges are handled via structured manual search and attorney inputs.
