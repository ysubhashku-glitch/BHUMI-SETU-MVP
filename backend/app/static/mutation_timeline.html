Statutory Notice: Bihar Land Reforms & Mutation Precept

Per the Supreme Court of India in Suraj Bhan v. Financial Commissioner (2007) 6 SCC 186, mutation entries (दाखिल-खारिज) are fiscal records maintained solely for land revenue collection. A sanctioned mutation or issued Shuddhi Patra (शुद्धि पत्र) does NOT confer, establish, or extinguish legal ownership or title. Absence of a recorded mutation does not prove unencumbered ownership.
