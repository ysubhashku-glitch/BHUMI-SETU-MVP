01 • THE PROBLEM

The Trillion-Dollar Land Trust Deficit in India

Land decisions are paralyzed by the disconnect between 6 conflicting realities.

☗
BHUMI SETU
1. LEGAL REALITY

Ancestral chains, fraudulent power of attorneys, unprobated wills, and missing root registry deeds.

2. GOVT RECORD REALITY

Outdated Jamabandis, stuck Dakhil Kharij (mutation) files, and colonial cadastral map discrepancies.

3. PHYSICAL GROUND REALITY

Boundary ridge (*aal*) shifts, missing corner pins, and actual measured area being 5–15% less than deed area.

4. POSSESSION REALITY

Encroachment by third parties, adverse possession claims, and non-resident absentee landowner blindspots.

5. REGULATORY RESTRICTIONS

Strict tribal tenancy restrictions (CNT/SPT Act in Jharkhand), Land Ceiling limits, and DC sanction requirements.

6. TRANSACTION REALITY

Absence of motorable road access, active CrPC 144 stay orders, and un-mortgageable land parcels.
