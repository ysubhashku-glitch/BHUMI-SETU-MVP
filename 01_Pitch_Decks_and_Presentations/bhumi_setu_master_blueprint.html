Data Processing Pipeline (End-to-End Lifecycle)

Extract Jamabandi, Khatiyan, registered deeds via instant portal sync & upload.

STEP 2 • FIELD SURVEY

Field Amin logs GPS pins (P1..P4), Chauhadri occupant verification, and road width.

STEP 3 • TRUTH DELTA

Calculate area delta ($\Delta_{area}$), boundary variance, and tribal CNT/SPT restrictions.

STEP 4 • PASSPORT

Generate tamper-evident Land Passport with QR code and publish to Property Graph.

Spatial Discrepancy Formulation

$$\Delta_{\text{area}} = \frac{\text{GPS Area} - \text{Deed Area}}{\text{Deed Area}} \times 100$$

• $|\Delta| \le 4\% \implies$ Customary variance (Clean)
• $4\% < |\Delta| \le 10\% \implies$ Warning (+15 Risk Score)
• $|\Delta| > 10\% \implies$ Severe boundary deficit (+30 Risk Score)

The 6-Dimension Deterministic Risk Engine

1. Title & Lineage: Sale deed vs Khatiyan chain match.
2. Spatial Variance: Measured vs Jamabandi acre delta.
3. Tenancy Laws: CNT/SPT Act tribal transfer restriction.
4. Mutation Compliance: Dakhil Kharij + Lagan receipt audit.
5. Physical Encroachment: Chauhadri ridge conflicts.
6. Access & Litigation: Motorable road & CrPC 144 checks.
